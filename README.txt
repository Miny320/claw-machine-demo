CLAW MACHINE DEMO

HOW TO OPEN
Unzip the folder.
Double-click index.html.
It opens in your browser (Chrome, Edge or Safari).
It needs no install, no login and no internet.

HOW TO PLAY
Move the claw with the yellow arrow buttons.
You can also use the left and right arrow keys, or tap inside the glass.
Press the red DROP button (or the space bar) to drop the claw.
The claw grabs the toy below it and carries it to the prize door.

EXTRAS
The top right corner has a full screen button and a sound button.
The top left corner counts the prizes won.
The game fits any screen size, from a Chromebook to an interactive whiteboard.

HOW IT IS MADE
Everything is in the one file index.html.
The machine, the claw and all motion are drawn in code.
The only picture is the soccer ball illustration.
No libraries and no outside assets are used.
